Read before every engagement. Append after every engagement.

## Run 1: Monorail / Signal (12 June 2026, World Cup window)

Corrections from Charubak that are now rules in SKILL.md:
1. Multi-product brands need separate guidelines per product (Monorail vs Signal): the long-term product job and the event sprint are different marketing jobs.
2. Competitor set must be the nearest five fair peers, not the category giants. Polymarket/Kalshi out as benchmarks, in as one line of context.
3. Creator plans must fit a stated budget envelope ($20K of $50K here). Big names move to a free/affiliate tier, never silently dropped.
4. Documents were initially too thesis-like. Founder docs are 700-950 words, tables over prose, brief and thorough at once.
5. No em dashes anywhere, in any document, ever. Scan before delivery.
6. The non-crypto domain (football) needs research parity with the crypto side: creators, calendar, culture, regulators.
7. Ecosystem-native products (Monad) get an allies/co-marketing section, not just rivals.
8. Gambling-adjacent paid acquisition = surrogate marketing concept (free-entry contest, data brand, software framing), with geo/compliance gates.

Findings that landed: signal.xyz parked-domain discovery; the World Cup had already started ("approaching" was wrong); three-red-cards opener as perfect product-fit narrative.

Open items not yet built: creator outreach DM templates; analytics/attribution spec module; real rates database (estimates still benchmark-based); scheduled narrative radar.
